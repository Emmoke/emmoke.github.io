About Emmoke

Emmoke is the sole proprietorship of Sewar Khalil in Dessau-Roßlau, Germany, founded in 2026 as a small business. The focus is on technical drafting and digital building documentation. In addition, own programs are being developed – the first one, Emmoke Finanzplan, grew out of a personal Excel plan for job, business and household.

Principles: understandable solutions, data stays with the users, no advertising and no hidden costs.
