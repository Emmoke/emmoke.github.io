Services

Drawings, models and documentation to your specifications – for building owners, property owners and managers, and as support for architects and engineering offices. On-site appointments by arrangement.

As-built plans & measurements

Survey of existing buildings and drawing as floor plans, sections and elevations – e.g. for conversion, sale, letting or insurance.

Working and construction drawings

Floor plans, sections, elevations and details according to the specifications of architects and engineering offices.

Formwork, reinforcement, steel and precast drawings

Construction drawings according to the structural engineer's specifications – with SOFiCAD, Tekla Structures and Revit.

BIM models

3D building models with component information in Revit and Tekla Structures – exchange e.g. via IFC.

Digitising existing buildings

Transfer old paper and scanned plans into CAD, model existing halls and buildings.

Visualisation & animation

Images and videos of 3D models and construction sequences with Twinmotion – for presentation and coordination.

Area and quantity take-off

Areas, volumes and quantities from plans, models or measurements – clearly documented.

Digital building documentation

Digitise, organise and keep existing building documents up to date – plans, areas and records in one place.

Note: building draftsman (Bauzeichner) is a recognised vocational qualification in Germany. Structural design and building permit submissions remain reserved for engineers and architects – I create the drawings and models in cooperation with them or according to their specifications.

About Emmoke

Emmoke is the sole proprietorship of Sewar Khalil, qualified building draftsman and BIM designer, in Dessau-Roßlau, Germany – founded in 2026 as a small business. The focus is on plans, BIM models and digital building documentation. In addition, own programs are being developed – the first one, Emmoke Finanzplan, grew out of a personal Excel plan for job, business and household.

Principles: understandable solutions, data stays with the users, no advertising and no hidden costs.
