What I can do for you

Three client groups, three focus areas – choose what fits you.

For architects & engineering offices

More CAD/BIM work than capacity? I support you project by project.

  • Working and construction drawings
  • Formwork and reinforcement drawings to the structural engineer's specifications
  • Steel and precast drawings
  • BIM models in Revit and Tekla Structures, exchange via IFC

For owners & property managers

Old plans or none at all? I turn them into clean digital as-built plans.

  • On-site measurement in and around Dessau-Roßlau
  • Floor plans, sections, elevations
  • Digitising old paper and scanned plans (PDF, DWG)
  • Area calculation and digital building documentation

For contractors & businesses

Construction drawings and documentation that work on site.

  • Construction and detail drawings
  • 3D models of halls, steel and precast buildings
  • Quantity and area take-off
  • Visualisation and animation with Twinmotion

Note: Emmoke provides drafting and design CAD/BIM services based on existing specifications. Structural calculations, permit planning and acting as design author (Entwurfsverfasser) are not offered and remain reserved for the planners qualified and responsible for them.
