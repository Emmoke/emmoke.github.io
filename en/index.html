Services as a building draftsman

Careful drafting and documentation work to your specifications – for building owners, property owners and managers, and as support for architects and engineering offices.

As-built plans & measurements

Survey of existing buildings and drawing as floor plans, sections and elevations – e.g. for conversion, sale, letting or insurance.

Working and construction drawings

Elaboration of floor plans, sections, elevations and details according to the specifications of architects and engineering offices.

Digitising old plans

Transfer paper and scanned plans cleanly into CAD – as DWG/DXF and PDF, to scale and labelled.

BIM models

Digital 3D building models with component information (e.g. in IFC format) – as a basis for planning, quantities and facility management.

Area and quantity take-off

Calculation of areas, volumes and quantities from plans or measurements – clearly documented.

Digital building documentation

Digitise, organise and keep existing building documents up to date – plans, areas and records in one place.

Note: building draftsman (Bauzeichner) is a recognised vocational qualification in Germany. Building permit submissions as design author, structural design and other services reserved for architects or engineers are not offered by me directly – but drafting support in cooperation with an architecture or engineering office is.

About Emmoke

Emmoke is the sole proprietorship of Sewar Khalil, qualified building draftsman, in Dessau-Roßlau, Germany – founded in 2026 as a small business. The focus is on technical drawings, as-built plans and digital building documentation. In addition, own programs are being developed – the first one, Emmoke Finanzplan, grew out of a personal Excel plan for job, business and household.

Principles: understandable solutions, data stays with the users, no advertising and no hidden costs.
